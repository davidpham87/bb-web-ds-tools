Let’s talk about your data. Specifically, let’s talk about where it goes.
Usually, when you use a "smart" tool, your data is packed up, shipped off to a
server farm in Northern Virginia, and fed into an algorithm that is desperately
trying to figure out how to sell you more targeted ads for socks.

We think that’s creepy. So we built something different.

## Datasets: The Excel Killer (We Wish)

First, we have the **Datasets Manager**. It’s like Excel, but without the
Clippy. You can drag and drop CSVs, TSVs, or JSON files.

You can edit cells! You can sort! You can filter!
It is the thrill of data entry, now in a web browser.
"But can it handle 10 million rows?"
No. It’s a browser. If you try to load 10 million rows, Chrome will eat your
RAM, burp, and crash. Keep it reasonable, folks.

## Visualization with Vega-Lite

Once you have your data, you probably want to look at it. We use **Vega-Lite**.
It allows you to describe charts using JSON.
Yes, you write code to make a chart.
"Why can't I just click a button?"
Because buttons are for the weak. JSON is for the strong. Also, it allows for
some truly beautiful, interactive visualizations that will distract your boss
from the fact that the sales numbers are down.

## Local AI with Gemma: The Introvert’s LLM

And finally, the pièce de résistance: **Local AI**.
Everyone is talking about AI. It’s the current "blockchain." But unlike your
cousin’s NFT collection, this is actually useful.

We integrated **Google's Gemma** model using MediaPipe. Here is the kicker:
**It runs on YOUR device.**

When you chat with this AI, your text isn't going to OpenAI, or Google, or the
NSA. It stays right there. You can ask it to summarize your secret diary, or
debug your proprietary code, or write fanfiction about toaster ovens. We don't
know. We can't see it.

It uses your GPU. If you have a potato for a computer, it will be slow. If you
have a gaming rig, it will be fast. That’s physics. We can't fix physics.

## Summary

So there you have it. Tools to manage your messy data and an AI that won't
snitch on you. What a time to be alive.
